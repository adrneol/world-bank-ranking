Use [chart adapters](../../../../frontend/src/components/charts/chartData.js), [TimeSeriesChart](../../../../frontend/src/components/charts/TimeSeriesChart.jsx), [BarComparisonChart](../../../../frontend/src/components/charts/BarComparisonChart.jsx), [IntervalLineChart](../../../../frontend/src/components/charts/IntervalLineChart.jsx), [SlopeChart](../../../../frontend/src/components/charts/SlopeChart.jsx), and [the shared table fallback](../../../../frontend/src/components/ui.jsx) where appropriate.

## Charts and history

- Use a line for a long annual history, grouped bars for discrete like-unit comparisons, and slope/interval displays for appropriate endpoint or segmented tasks. Bars need a meaningful zero baseline. A rate chart spanning positive and negative values should make zero easy to see. Preserve orientation for ranks and explain it.
- Make the effective date window explicit. Keep Overview's snapshot year distinct from the history window. Offer inclusive start/end controls and reasonable recent/full-history presets when requested. Only claim a filtered graph when rendered data and its table use the same window. Keep gaps inside the window and use the previous-year leg for backend YoY as needed.
- Select series by registered identities and capabilities. Total GDP and GDP per capita can each expose their registered bases. Separate current and constant US$ from current and constant PPP international dollars in labeled aligned panels. Group compatible annual CPI inflation, GDP-deflator inflation and backend real GDP growth on an annual-percent scale with distinct labels. CPI index uses its own base-year panel. Exports/imports may share current-US$ units. Preserve each primary level when adding context. Never put incompatible units on an unlabeled shared scale or use normalization to disguise an incompatible comparison.
- Nulls create line gaps or unavailable bars. Do not connect missing years or imply zero. Match tooltip, table, legend and axis units, signs, precision and period labels. Use color plus dashes, point shapes or direct labels. Plot only meaningful points/ticks and use a labeled, focusable horizontal scroll region for long mobile histories. Make touch/keyboard inspection and data-table equivalents available. [Recharts accessibility guidance](https://recharts.github.io/en-US/guide/accessibility/)

## Tables and Movement

- Preserve every relevant column. Use semantic headers, captions, right-aligned tabular numbers, readable signs and unit labels. Keep horizontal overflow in the table's named region, retain keyboard scroll, and use a sticky first column when row identity matters. For long tables use a bounded two-axis scrollport and opaque sticky headers. Offset multiple header rows independently and verify the sticky corner and page header layering. Expose raw observations, explanations and unavailable reasons without depending on hover.
- Keep search, sorting, filters, selected row, coverage and comparator semantics. Where a mobile disclosure replaces a wide inline view, expose the same evidence in reading order and preserve equivalent keyboard navigation. Check [YearlyTable](../../../../frontend/src/sections/YearlyTable.jsx), [Compare](../../../../frontend/src/sections/Compare.jsx) and [rank verification](../../../../frontend/src/sections/LevelVerification.jsx).
- For [RankMovement](../../../../frontend/src/sections/RankMovement.jsx) and the specialist Movement views, lead with the analytical conclusion and key result, then measurement basis, period and comparator. Follow with a suitable chart and concise interpretation. Make observations, rank or growth decomposition, methods, eligible and common universes, exclusions, vintage, coverage and source evidence visible or discoverable through named disclosures. Preserve the difference between a derived ranking and the World Bank observations that support it.
