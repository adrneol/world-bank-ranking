## Observation identity and release evidence

- WDI is source 2. Validate the exact indicator code and source metadata, the World Bank entity identity, and the requested annual interval. The audited series client did not explicitly send source=2 and selected the first metadata row. For changes in this area verify the request source, returned identities where present, metadata, page IDs and totals, unique entity–indicator–year keys, and coherent source release evidence. A complete row count alone does not prove those properties.
- Current ingestion coerces numeric/date fields before classification. For validation work, define accepted wire shapes, validate the whole annual token and range, and keep legitimate finite signed values and explicit zero. Reject malformed strings, booleans, duplicates and unexpected series or years according to a documented publication policy. Preserve null/absence semantics and the relevant rejection/coverage counts. Never interpolate, substitute another indicator, or turn missing into zero.
- Observations are keyed by country_id, indicator_id and year. REAL supports existing ranking math. value_raw is evidence for the accepted value and may represent the post-JSON-parse decimal rather than the original wire lexeme. If original lexical precision is required, retain validated upstream text or a versioned raw artifact. Explain the actual storage guarantee.
- Keep WDI lastupdated, per-row retrieval time, successful check/publish time, content identity and the generation being served distinct. A revision can change a historical value, remove it, or advance provenance without changing the economic value. A narrow refresh can leave older vintages in untouched years. Report mixed or unknown vintage for the actual rows used. A maximum stamp alone cannot establish identical coverage or content.
- Validate official metadata against [World Bank indicator metadata](https://datahelpdesk.worldbank.org/knowledgebase/articles/898599-indicator-api-queries), [request parameters](https://datahelpdesk.worldbank.org/knowledgebase/articles/898581-api-basic-call-structures), [aggregate semantics](https://datahelpdesk.worldbank.org/knowledgebase/articles/898614-aggregate-api-queries) and [versioned data queries](https://datahelpdesk.worldbank.org/knowledgebase/articles/1886686-advanced-data-api-queries). Treat saved fixtures as vintage-bound evidence.

## Dimensional checks

Trace permitted operations and units through [transforms](../../../../backend/src/domain/transforms.js), [YoY](../../../../backend/src/domain/yoy.js), [Compare plans](../../../../backend/src/domain/compareAnalysis.js) and [Compare responses](../../../../backend/src/services/entityCompare.js).

- Keep current US$, constant 2015 US$, current international dollars, and constant 2021 international dollars distinct. Preserve the base and currency for both total GDP and per-capita levels. Never equate or silently normalize, rebase, convert or aggregate incompatible measures.
- Annual real GDP growth is application-derived here from adjacent annual NY.GDP.MKTP.KD observations. Preserve the backend's previous-year requirement and positive base check. CPI inflation and GDP-deflator inflation are distinct WDI annual-rate series. Real GDP growth may share their annual-percent scale with clear labels and source attribution. CPI index (2010 = 100) uses its own panel.
- Format an operation's output from its declared unit. A GDP absolute delta retains the money basis, while its relative growth and CAGR use percent. A difference between rates uses percentage points, and an index level delta uses index points. Keep annualized versus cumulative results, shares of GDP, ordered FX units and signed-flow behavior explicit. Verify interval inclusion and all required legs before summing, averaging or calculating a ratio of sums.
- [Ranking](../../../../backend/src/domain/ranking.js) filters invalid rows, applies the registry's direction and breaks exact numeric ties by ISO3 ascending, then assigns ordinal positions. Neutral metrics have no rank. Preserve the year/indicator-specific valid-observation denominator. Official aggregates can carry published observations but remain outside ranked economy sets. [Compare universe](../../../../backend/src/domain/compareUniverse.js) distinguishes observed, common/complete and reference sets. Keep published aggregates and application-derived groups distinguishable, disclose incomplete coverage, and block dependent comparisons where the contract requires it.
- When validating Compare, assert the exact value, unit, observation or derivation flag, methodology, comparator, coverage and visible display. Check cards, interval and gap rows, chart labels and data-table equivalents. Current metric-key-based currency formatting is a known audit finding. Skill setup does not change it.
